# Payment Plans - Karachi Homes Realty

NOTE: These are DEMO plans created for a portfolio project. They are NOT real offers. Real plans differ by project and change over time.

## Plan A: Full Payment (Cash / Bank Transfer)

- Typical for ready houses and resale plots in DHA.
- 100 percent of the price paid within 2 to 4 weeks of signing the sale agreement.
- Often allows a small negotiation on price.
- Possession: immediately after transfer for ready properties.

## Plan B: Installment Plan - Bahria Town Plot (Demo)

- Down payment: 25 percent of the price.
- Balance: 24 equal monthly installments.
- Possession: after the final installment, or earlier if the development is complete and dues are cleared.
- Late fee: demo surcharge applies on delayed installments.

Example: A plot priced at 80 lakh.
- Down payment: 20 lakh.
- Remaining: 60 lakh over 24 months, about 2.5 lakh per month.

## Plan C: Installment Plan - Bahria Town House (Demo)

- Down payment: 30 percent of the price.
- Balance: 36 monthly installments.
- Possession: on completion of payments or as per the project's schedule.

Example: A house priced at 3 crore.
- Down payment: 90 lakh.
- Remaining: 2.1 crore over 36 months, about 5.83 lakh per month.

## Plan D: Askari 5 Developer Plan (Demo)

- Down payment: 20 percent.
- Balance: 18 monthly installments.
- Possession: 6 months after the final installment.

## Plan E: Short Plan for Flats (Demo)

- Down payment: 35 percent.
- Balance: 12 monthly installments.
- Suitable for apartments in buildings like Bahria Heights.

## Plan F: Bank Financing (Demo)

- Home financing may be available from banks for eligible buyers.
- Typical structure: the buyer pays a down payment (often 20 to 30 percent) and the bank finances the rest over several years.
- Approval depends on the bank's policy, income proof and the property.
- Karachi Homes Realty does not approve loans. We help with documents.

## Rules That Apply to All Plans

- Token money is deducted from the down payment.
- All payments should be made through bank channels and receipts kept.
- Agreement terms must be read and signed before the first installment.
- Delay in payments may lead to surcharge or cancellation, depending on the terms.
- Cancellation refund rules are in the company policy document.

## Which Plan Is Best?

- Buyer with full funds: Plan A, may get a better price.
- Buyer with limited funds and time: Plans B, C or D.
- Buyer for flat: Plan E.
- Buyer needing a loan: Plan F.
