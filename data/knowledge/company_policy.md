# Company Policy - Karachi Homes Realty

NOTE: Fictional company created for a portfolio project. All details are demo.

## About Us
Karachi Homes Realty is a real estate agency in Karachi helping clients buy, sell and rent plots, houses and flats in DHA, Bahria Town, Askari 5, Scheme 33, Gulistan-e-Jauhar, North Nazimabad and Malir.

## Office Timings
- Monday to Saturday: 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed for walk-ins. Site visits on Sunday are by prior appointment only.
- Public holidays: Closed. Online inquiries are answered on the next working day.

## Contact Channels
- Website inquiry form
- WhatsApp
- Phone
All inquiries are answered within one working day. Hot inquiries are handled faster.

## Site Visit Policy
- Site visits are free.
- Visits are booked at least 24 hours in advance.
- Visit slots: 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM.
- Each visit lasts about one hour.
- Rescheduling is free if requested at least 3 hours before.
- A visit does not oblige the client to buy.

## Commission Policy
- Sales: 1 percent from the buyer and 1 percent from the seller, payable only on a completed sale.
- Rentals: one month's rent, paid once on a signed rental agreement.
- No commission is charged if the deal does not complete.

## Token Money and Refunds
- Token money is recorded with a written receipt.
- If the property documents fail verification, the token money is refunded in full within 7 working days.
- If the buyer cancels without a valid reason, token money may be forfeited, as per the agreement.
- Refund terms for installment plans follow the signed project agreement.

## Document Verification
We help verify ownership documents, society records and dues. Final legal verification should be done by the client's own lawyer.

## Privacy
Client information is used only to handle the inquiry. We do not sell personal data.

## What We Do Not Do
- We do not guarantee profit or future price increases.
- We do not give legal or tax advice. Please consult a qualified professional.
- We do not approve bank loans.

## Escalation to a Human Agent
The assistant must connect the client to a human agent when:
- The client asks for a final price, discount or negotiation.
- The question is about legal disputes or exact tax rates.
- The client is upset, or asks for a person.
- The answer is not available in our documents.
