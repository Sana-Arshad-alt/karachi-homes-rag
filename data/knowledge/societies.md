# Societies Guide - Karachi Homes Realty

NOTE: DEMO summary for a portfolio project. General descriptions only. Verify current rules, charges and availability with the society office.

## DHA Karachi (Defence Housing Authority)

### Overview
DHA is a large, established planned community in south Karachi, popular with buyers who want developed infrastructure, security and a premium address. It is divided into phases. Phases 5, 6, 7 and 8 are among the most requested.

### Popular Areas
- DHA Phase 5: mostly developed, well-known for houses and commercial areas.
- DHA Phase 6: popular for houses and bungalows, includes areas like Creek Vista.
- DHA Phase 7 and 8: larger plots, more bungalows and investment plots, close to the coast.

### Typical Property Types
Houses and bungalows (120 sq yd to 1000 sq yd), plots, and apartments in some buildings.

### Price Level
Generally the highest in our listings. Prices vary strongly by phase and location.

### Transfer
Transfers go through the society office. A No Objection Certificate (NOC) and clearance of dues are required before transfer.

### Good For
Families wanting an established area, and buyers looking for long-term value.

## Bahria Town Karachi

### Overview
Bahria Town Karachi is a large gated community on the Super Highway side, divided into precincts. It offers many installment options and a wide range of property sizes.

### Popular Areas
- Precincts (for example Precinct 10-A, 11-A, 27, 31): residential plots and houses.
- Bahria Homes and Iqbal Villas: smaller houses, lower budget.
- Bahria Heights: apartments.
- Bahria Sports City: sports and lifestyle area.

### Typical Property Types
Plots and houses (125 sq yd, 250 sq yd, 500 sq yd are common), apartments, and files in newer precincts.

### Price Level
More affordable entry prices than DHA, with installment plans available on many projects.

### Things to Check
Possession date, development status of the precinct, and pending dues. Buyers should visit the site before paying.

### Good For
Buyers who want installments, newer homes, and a lower starting budget. The commute to central Karachi is longer.

## Askari 5

### Overview
Askari 5 is a housing scheme in the Malir Cantonment area, with sectors such as Sector J. It has a high number of listings and mid-range prices.

### Typical Property Types
Houses and plots in sectors, in sizes such as 120 sq yd and 240 sq yd.

### Price Level
Mid-range, generally below DHA.

### Good For
Buyers looking for a mid-budget house in a secured scheme.

## Scheme 33

### Overview
Scheme 33 is a large residential scheme in east Karachi with a mix of plots, houses and developing areas.

### Typical Property Types
Plots and houses, with many small sized plots.

### Price Level
Budget to mid-range.

### Things to Check
Development status of the specific block, utilities, and clear ownership papers.

## Gulistan-e-Jauhar

### Overview
A well-known established area in Karachi with many apartments and flats, close to shops, schools and main roads.

### Typical Property Types
Flats and apartments mainly, some houses.

### Good For
Buyers wanting an established location with city facilities, and flats at a lower budget.

## North Nazimabad

### Overview
An older, well-established residential area in Karachi with houses and flats, schools, markets and good road access.

### Typical Property Types
Houses on small to mid-sized plots, flats.

### Good For
Families wanting an established neighborhood.

## Malir

### Overview
Malir is a large area in east Karachi that includes several schemes and residential projects. Prices vary widely by project.

### Things to Check
Which scheme the property is in, and whether the project is approved.

## General Tips for Any Society
- Always verify ownership documents with the society office.
- Check that there are no pending dues or disputes.
- Visit the property in person before paying.
- Compare similar properties in the same area before agreeing to a price.
- Get all agreements in writing.
