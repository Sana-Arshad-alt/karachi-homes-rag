# Karachi Homes Realty - Frequently Asked Questions

NOTE: This is DEMO data for a portfolio project. Fees, taxes and rules are approximate and change often. Always confirm with the relevant authority before any real transaction.

## Buying Basics

### What documents do I need to buy a property?
A buyer usually needs a copy of CNIC, passport size photographs, and proof of funds (bank statement or pay order). For overseas Pakistanis, a copy of NICOP or passport is needed. The seller provides the original file or allotment letter, ownership documents, and a clean transfer record.

### What is token money?
Token money is a small advance paid to reserve a property while documents are verified. It is usually a small percentage of the price. It is recorded in a written receipt. Refund of token money depends on the agreement signed.

### What is the usual buying process?
1. Select property and visit the site.
2. Agree on price and pay token money.
3. Verify documents with the society and the seller.
4. Sign the sale agreement and pay the agreed advance.
5. Pay remaining amount and complete the transfer at the society office or registrar.
6. Take possession.

### How long does a property transfer take?
In societies like DHA and Bahria Town, a clean transfer usually takes from one to several weeks. Delays happen if documents are incomplete, dues are unpaid, or NOC is pending.

### What is an NOC?
A No Objection Certificate (NOC) is issued by the society to confirm that the property has no pending dues or disputes and can be transferred.

### What is a plot file versus a developed plot?
A file is a booking document for a plot in a scheme where possession or development may be pending. A developed plot has roads and utilities and can be built on. Files are usually cheaper but carry more risk.

### What is the difference between a plot, a house and a flat?
A plot is empty land. A house is a built residential unit on its own land. A flat is a unit in a multi-storey building, such as Bahria Heights.

## Size and Price

### What is a square yard (gaz) and how does it relate to square feet?
One square yard equals 9 square feet. A 120 sq yd plot is 1,080 sq ft. A 500 sq yd plot is 4,500 sq ft, and is commonly called a 500 gaz plot.

### What do lakh and crore mean?
One lakh is 100,000 rupees. One crore is 10,000,000 rupees (100 lakh). For example 4.5 crore is 45,000,000 rupees.

### Are the listed prices final?
No. Listed prices are asking prices. The final price is agreed between buyer and seller and can change with market conditions.

### Do you list prices for all properties?
Our listings show the asking price. For properties where the price is not listed, a sales agent will share it on request.

## Costs and Fees

### What costs come with buying a property besides the price?
Typical extra costs are transfer fee, stamp duty, taxes at the time of registration, society charges, and agent commission. The exact amounts depend on the property value and current government rates.

### How much is the agent commission?
Under our demo company policy, Karachi Homes Realty charges 1 percent from the buyer and 1 percent from the seller on completed sales. Rental commission is one month's rent.

### Who pays the transfer fee?
Normally the buyer and seller agree on this before signing. Many societies charge a transfer fee, and government taxes apply separately.

### Are there taxes on buying property?
Yes. Federal and provincial taxes and registration charges apply, and rates change with each budget. Filer and non-filer status can affect the rate. Please confirm the current rates with a tax advisor before buying.

### Can I pay in cash?
Large property payments are generally expected through banking channels (pay order, bank transfer). Please speak to a sales agent about the safest payment method.

## Installments and Financing

### Do you offer installment plans?
Some projects, such as Bahria Town and Askari 5 developer plans, offer installments. See the payment plans document for demo examples.

### Can I get a bank mortgage?
Some banks offer home financing in Pakistan for eligible buyers. Eligibility depends on income, property type and bank policy. Our agents can guide you to banks, but approval is the bank's decision.

### What happens if I miss an installment?
Late payment may attract a surcharge, and repeated delays may lead to cancellation under the project's terms. Always read the payment schedule before signing.

## Societies

### Which areas do you cover?
We mainly cover DHA Karachi, Bahria Town Karachi, Askari 5, Scheme 33, Gulistan-e-Jauhar, North Nazimabad and Malir.

### What is the difference between DHA and Bahria Town?
DHA is an older, established society in central-south Karachi, with generally higher prices. Bahria Town Karachi is a newer large gated community on the Super Highway side, with more installment options and lower entry prices. See the societies document for more.

### Is Bahria Town far from the city?
Bahria Town is outside the main city on the Super Highway and typically takes a longer commute to central Karachi than DHA.

## Renting

### Do you also handle rentals?
Yes. We handle residential rentals in our covered areas. Commission for rental is one month's rent, paid once on a successful agreement.

### What documents are needed to rent a property?
A copy of CNIC, a recent photograph, and employment or business proof. A rental agreement is signed and a security deposit is usually taken.

### How much security deposit is normal?
Commonly one to two months' rent, depending on the owner.

## Investment

### Is real estate a safe investment?
Property can hold value over time, but prices can fall or stay flat, and selling can take months. No agent can guarantee profit. Please do your own research.

### Should I buy a plot or a house for investment?
Plots need less money upfront and can be flipped, but carry development and possession risk. Houses give rental income. The right choice depends on your budget and timeline.
